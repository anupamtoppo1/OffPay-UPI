Why use us

Designed for the signal you have.

Useful in a basement, on a slow connection, or with a simple phone. OffPay is a payment preparation tool, not a bank.

Private by design

QR and form values are processed in your browser. Nothing is sent to an OffPay server.

No install required

Open it from a link on any modern browser. The camera is used only after you allow it.

Works without mobile data

*99# uses your mobile network's signaling channel, so it does not need internet data.

You stay in control

OffPay cannot move money or see your PIN. Your bank's secure flow completes the payment.

OffPay UPIOffline-ready does not mean network-free: *99# needs cellular signal.
